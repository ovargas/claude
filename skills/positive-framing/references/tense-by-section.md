# Tense by Section

Governing tense per section in `/proposal` and `/idea`. One tense per section; shift only on a real time-reference change.

## Mechanical heuristic (callout)

**For present perfect:** if removing the present perfect and substituting simple past or simple present preserves the meaning, switch. If the sentence loses its continuing-relevance bridge — typically because it cites a track record with a concrete anchor — keep present perfect.

- "We have been delivering services for years." → drop the bridge → "We deliver [these] services." (Switch.)
- "We have helped 40+ fintech companies since 2018." → anchor is irreplaceable → keep.

## /proposal sections

| Section | Governing tense | Allowed | Not allowed | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Executive Summary | Simple present | Active voice statements of value | Vague present perfect, future-tense hedging | Before: "We have been helping companies modernize." → After: "We help companies modernize." |
| Scope of Work | Simple present | "We deliver", "the engagement includes" | Present perfect, present-perfect-continuous | Before: "We have been conducting discovery..." → After: "We conduct discovery..." |
| Methodology | Simple present | Step-by-step active descriptions | Vague past or future | Before: "Phase 1 will be focused on..." → After: "Phase 1 covers..." |
| Timeline | Simple future or present-as-future | "Phase 1 begins June 3", "delivery ships in week 6" | Past, vague present perfect | Before: "Delivery will have been completed by week 6." → After: "Delivery ships in week 6." |
| Track Record / Credentials | **Anchored present perfect (default)** + simple past | "We have helped 40+ companies"; "we built X in 2024" | Vague present perfect, present-perfect-continuous | Before: "We have been working with enterprises." → After: "We have built integrations for 12 enterprises since 2022." |
| Cost | Simple present | Direct rates and totals | Conditional or hedging modals | Before: "The cost could potentially be around $X." → After: "The fixed cost is $X." |
| Assumptions | Simple present | Literal assumption statements | Editorial softening | Pass through unchanged. |
| Not Included | Pass through | Negation is the function | — | Do not edit. |
| Risks | Pass through | Threat language by design | — | Do not edit. |

## /idea sections

| Section | Governing tense | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Problem Statement | Simple present | Name the current pain in active, present-tense terms. |
| Value Proposition (Before/After) | Simple present (After block), simple present (Before block) | Both blocks are stative descriptions, not narratives. |
| MVP Scope (Must Have) | Simple present | Bullets describe capabilities, not roadmap. |
| Open Questions | Simple present | Question form is fine; avoid present perfect. |
| Not Doing | Pass through | Negation is the function. |
| Risks and Assumptions | Pass through | Do not edit. |
| Current Alternatives (table) | Pass through | Tables are excluded structurally. |
| Business Model | Pass through | Factual section; outside this skill's scope. |

## Cross-section consistency check

After applying section-by-section rules, scan the document for tense shifts within a single paragraph. A paragraph that opens in simple present and ends in present perfect — without a genuine time-reference change — gets re-anchored to the section's governing tense.
